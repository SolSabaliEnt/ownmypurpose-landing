Frequently asked questions

Good questions before you invest.

Do I need a technical background?

No. The process is designed for founders who know the problem they want to solve, not necessarily how to build the software.

What if I only have a rough idea?

That's okay. A short fit conversation helps determine whether a focused planning engagement is useful or whether more business validation should happen first.

What do I receive for $1,250?

A defined product brief, first-version priorities, one core user journey, up to five low-fidelity wireframes, technical direction, build milestones, a preliminary estimate, and a delivery walkthrough with one consolidated revision round.

Can I take the Blueprint to another developer?

Yes—the intention is for the agreed planning deliverables to be independently useful. Specific ownership and licensing terms will be confirmed in the agreement before purchase.

Is Blueprint payment refundable?

Payment, cancellation, and refund terms are provided in the written agreement before you purchase. No automatic refund or guarantee is implied.

Who’s This For?

For businesses whose processes have outgrown their tools.

This is for business owners, operators, and experienced founders who already understand how their business creates value but need help determining how technology should support, preserve, or expand that value.

Your business operates through disconnected forms, spreadsheets, messages, calendars, and software
A valuable process still depends heavily on you or a key employee
You started building with Base44, Bubble, another no-code platform, or an AI app builder but cannot finish or stabilize the result
Your current software forces the business to work around its limitations
You have developed a repeatable method that could become a client portal, internal platform, licensed system, or SaaS product
You want an honest determination of whether custom software is actually justified
Not for unvalidated ideas, speculative app concepts with no operating business, or people seeking the cheapest possible build before clarifying the problem.

What Happens During the Fit Check?

A short conversation to determine whether a deeper assessment is warranted.

Fifteen minutes is not enough to design your entire system. It is enough to identify whether there is a meaningful business and technology problem, whether my approach fits it, and what the appropriate next step should be.

01

You explain the business, its customer, and the process you are trying to improve or preserve.

02

We identify the visible friction in the current tools, workflow, or unfinished application.

03

We discuss whether the likely direction is to buy, connect, repair, extend, build, or wait.

04

We determine whether the business qualifies for a complete Technology Ownership Assessment.

05

If there is a fit, I explain the assessment and next phase; if not, I give you the most responsible direction I can identify.

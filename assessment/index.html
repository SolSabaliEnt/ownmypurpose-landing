Framework

Seven dimensions, assessed in order.

01 / Function02 / Input03 / Parts04 / Structure05 / Operations06 / Regulation07 / Preservation

Examples of workflows

Inquiry to booking, job to invoice, scheduling to fulfillment, or service to repeat customer.

Outside this assessment

Custom code, full-company audits, extensive migration, tool licensing, implementation, and ongoing support require separate scope.

Evidence standards

Separate facts from assumptions.

01

Observed

Directly supported by agreed records or demonstrations.

02

Reported / Estimated

Information or assumptions identified explicitly as such.

03

Unknown

What remains unverified and would affect recommendations.

No guaranteed ROI, additional revenue, or cost reduction.

When it makes sense

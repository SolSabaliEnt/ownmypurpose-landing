MY STORY

“I couldn't control the system I was born into. But I could learn how it worked—and engineer a different outcome.”

I was raised in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, where I faced challenges early in life that would ultimately shape how I viewed the world. As I grew older, I began to understand that many of those challenges weren't simply a matter of individual circumstances. There were larger systems at work, influencing opportunity, access, and the outcomes people like me were expected to experience.

That realization changed my perspective. I understood that overcoming my circumstances required more than determination. I needed to understand how systems operated, who controlled them, and how they could be redesigned to produce different results. Rather than direct my energy toward resentment, I chose to focus on ownership, knowledge, and creating my own opportunities.

Over time, that thinking became the foundation of my approach to life, entrepreneurship, and eventually technology. I began looking at systems not simply as barriers to overcome, but as structures that could be studied, challenged, and reengineered.

My mission today is to create different outcomes for myself and others who have faced similar barriers. Not simply by navigating existing systems more successfully, but by challenging the assumptions behind them and building alternatives that expand access, opportunity, and ownership. I believe meaningful change happens when we move beyond recognizing what isn't working and begin creating what should exist instead.

Own My Purpose is the expression of that philosophy: understanding the system, taking ownership of my direction, and building a different outcome.

Frequently asked questions

Good questions before you invest.

Do I need a technical background?

No. The process is designed for founders who know the problem they want to solve, not necessarily how to build the software.

How much does full development cost?

Build cost is quoted against an agreed scope, complexity, integrations, timeline, and acceptance criteria. There is no responsible single fixed price for every MVP.

How long does an MVP take?

Development duration depends on scope. The Blueprint target is 5–7 business days after kickoff and complete inputs; custom development receives its own milestone schedule.

Who owns the code after the build?

Source code, third-party accounts, intellectual property, and deployment handoff are covered in the written development agreement. There is no blanket ownership promise before those terms are agreed.

Can you help with an existing app?

Potentially. If you already have an unfinished app or no-code prototype, share its current state during the fit conversation so the right next step can be recommended.
